About the Club

The Chess Club at CSUSB brings together students who enjoy the game, from complete beginners to tournament players. The club had been inactive for a while, so our officers went through the process of reactivating it and collected the university's chess sets and clocks. Today we meet regularly to play, learn and have fun.

Our mission is to build a friendly chess community on campus, teach the game to anyone who wants to learn, and give experienced players a place to compete.
